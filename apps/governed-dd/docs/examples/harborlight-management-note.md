# Harborlight HVAC Services: fictional management note

Scenario: fictional-harborlight-v1. Prepared solely for a software trial.
All company figures, statements and plans below are invented. Harborlight is
not a real target. Public industry research must not identify this business.

## Company and investment question

Harborlight supplies residential and commercial HVAC repair, equipment
installation and maintenance contracts in the United States. The fictional
investment question is whether maintenance expansion supports a credible
growth plan while preserving commercial quality.

## Management's statements

Management says revenue grew 25% in 2025 and maintenance contracts represented
30% of 2025 sales. Check these statements against the supplied revenue file.
Management expects replacement demand to benefit from an aging equipment base
and changing refrigerant requirements. These are hypotheses requiring external
evidence; no local equipment-age survey is supplied.

Management believes maintenance sales are recurring. There are no signed
contracts, renewal histories, cancellation terms or deferred-revenue schedules
in this trial data room. A segment label alone cannot establish recurrence.

## 2026 plan and commercial risks

Management's illustrative 2026 revenue target is USD14.0 million, compared with
the 2025 actual figures in the revenue file. No price/volume bridge, backlog,
technician staffing plan or acquisition plan is supplied.

Potential constraints are skilled-technician availability, customer budgets,
equipment/refrigerant transition costs and competitive pricing. Determine what
public evidence supports and what remains unknown at company level.

## Data definitions and limitations

Revenue in the CSV is recognized annual revenue, in USD millions. Gross margin
is gross profit divided by revenue. Segment gross profit is revenue multiplied
by the segment margin. Aggregate margin must be revenue-weighted. Gross profit
is not EBITDA. No overhead, cash conversion, customer-level data, geographic
mix, monthly seasonality or price/volume split is supplied.

Treat the CSV as the supplied numerical record for this exercise and this note
as management assertions to test. Preserve both originals. The deliberate
inconsistencies are part of the trial; do not silently correct this note.
